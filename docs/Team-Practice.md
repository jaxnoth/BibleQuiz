# Team practice

Coach-oriented session blocks. Modes marked future are not built in the app yet.

## Application

- Warm up with a short read-through of the day's chapter in Scripture.
- Clarify which question types you will emphasize (often General and According To early in the season).

## Study

- Split into pairs: one reads, one answers from memory verses and unique words.
- Use Flash Cards and Fill in the Blank for memory work.
- Use Scripture highlights (memory bands + unique words) for visual review.

## Play

- Run official-style **20-question rounds** in Quiz Practice (`round` + all types) so quotas match meet mix.
- Add Jeopardy for team energy; Situation Challenge for who/when/where drills.
- Future coach Play modes (rotation, live scoring, team buzzer) remain backlog.

## Jumping

- Buzzer practice builds early-jump timing without requiring a full meet setup.
- Pre-jump trainers (ending removed / timing option) are future features.
- Remind quizzers that Context and Quote types punish guessing from incomplete stems.
