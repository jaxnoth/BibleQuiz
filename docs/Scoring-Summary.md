# Scoring summary

Internal notes for understanding meet scoring while practicing in BibleQuiz. Confirm with your league rulebook.

## Toss-up questions

- The first quizzer to jump correctly typically scores the toss-up points for their team.
- An incorrect toss-up answer usually opens a bonus opportunity for the other team (league-specific).

## Bonus questions

- Bonus questions are often read to the opposing team after a missed toss-up.
- Bonus correctness is scored separately from the original toss-up.

## Quiz-out

- A quizzer who answers a set number of questions correctly may quiz out (leave active play).
- Exact quiz-out thresholds and replacements follow league rules.

## Penalties

- Fouls and errors can subtract points or remove jump rights for a question.
- Practice games in this app track self-scored correct / needs-review only; they do not apply live penalties.

## App practice modes

| Mode | Scoring in app |
|---|---|
| 20-question round | Self-score correct / needs review |
| Continuous | Same |
| Buzzer practice | Self-score after buzz + reveal |
| Speed round | Count of self-scored corrects in 60 seconds |
| Jeopardy | Coach awards team points |
