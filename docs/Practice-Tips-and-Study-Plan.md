# Practice tips and study plan

Individual study outline for Gospel of John chapters enabled in the app (currently 1-5). Adapt to your team calendar.

## Daily rhythm (Days 1-6)

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Read the chapter aloud once. Mark memory verses. |
| 2 | Drill memory verses (flash cards, fill-blank, verse scramble). |
| 3 | Unique-word hunt in Scripture reader (highlights on). |
| 4 | Quiz Practice: single-type filters (G, then A, then S/X/Q). |
| 5 | Full 20-question round (`all` types) plus buzzer practice. |
| 6 | Mixed review: weak questions from My Progress, speed round, Situation Challenge. |

## Tips

- Say answers out loud; silent reading hides jump timing gaps.
- When a Context (`X`) question misses, re-read the surrounding verses in Scripture.
- Use chapter-range practice before expanding to John 1-5.
- Track needs-review items; clear a few each session instead of only running new rounds.

## Scripture attribution

Chapter text is ingested for **internal personal use**. Public distribution requires confirmed translation rights and attribution text.
